How it works

Connected in about a minute.

1

Install on your computer

Download above, then open it. It sits in the menu bar (macOS) or system tray (Windows) and shows a pairing QR code.

2

Allow it to control the computer

macOS asks for Accessibility permission once — that is what lets your phone move the pointer and type. Windows needs nothing.

3

Open the phone app

On the same Wi-Fi, your computer appears by itself. Tap it — or scan the QR code, or type the IP shown on screen.

4

Use it

Slide to move, tap to click, two fingers to scroll. The tabs along the bottom switch to keyboard, media and audio.

FAQ

Common questions.

Does it cost anything?

The app is free. Pro is an optional in-app purchase that adds Air Mouse (point your phone to move the cursor), Voice Focus for the microphone, and speaker equaliser presets.

Does my data leave my network?

No. Your phone talks directly to your computer over your local Wi-Fi, so trackpad input, keystrokes and audio never reach us — we run no servers for them. The app sends anonymous crash reports (you can switch them off) and checks this website for new versions. See the privacy policy.

Why does macOS ask for Accessibility permission?

macOS requires it for any app that moves the pointer or types on your behalf, and that is the only thing it is used for. If the Permissions screen still says “Not granted” after you switch it on, press Reset permission there — macOS sometimes ties the old permission to a previous copy of the app.

The microphone or speaker produces no sound. Why?

Audio routing needs a free virtual audio driver: BlackHole on macOS, VB-CABLE on Windows; the app offers to install it. Also pick that device as the microphone inside Zoom, Meet or Discord — those apps keep their own choice and ignore the system default. The guide walks through it.

Can I use it over the internet?

Not today. Both devices have to be on the same local network. Relaying over the internet would mean running a server, and keeping everything local is the point of the design.

Will it drain my phone battery?

Using it is roughly like watching a video — the screen is the biggest cost. The app keeps the screen awake while the trackpad is open so it doesn't lock under your finger.
