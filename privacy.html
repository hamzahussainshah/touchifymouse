Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-04-25 · Last updated: 2026-09-24 · Contact: aitools.deventiatech@gmail.com

This Privacy Policy describes how the TouchifyMouse mobile app ("the app", "we", "us") handles information when you use it. The app turns your phone into a wireless trackpad, keyboard, microphone, and remote speaker for a desktop computer running the TouchifyMouse desktop companion on the same local Wi-Fi network.

1. Summary

  • No account is needed. Signing in with Google is optional; if you do, we keep your name, email address and profile photo so you have an account (see §3), and you can delete it at any time.
  • We do not run our own servers.
  • All trackpad input, keystrokes, audio, and pairing data flow directly between your phone and your own desktop computer over your local Wi-Fi network. None of it leaves your network.
  • We do not sell, share, or transfer your personal information to third parties for their independent use.
  • The app contains no advertising and no analytics.
  • If the app crashes, it sends an anonymous crash report to Google Firebase Crashlytics so we can fix the problem. Crash reports never include what you type, click, or say, and you can turn them off in Settings → Privacy (see §3).

2. Permissions and how we use them

Camera — used only to scan the pairing QR code shown by the desktop companion app. Camera frames are processed on-device (by Google ML Kit on Android and Apple's Vision framework on iOS) and are not stored, recorded, transmitted, or sent anywhere. The camera is only active while the QR scan sheet is open.

Microphone — used only when you explicitly enable the "Use phone as microphone" feature. Audio captured from your phone's microphone is streamed in real time over your local Wi-Fi to the paired desktop computer that you own. Audio is not recorded, persisted, or sent to any third-party server. The microphone is silent until you toggle the feature on, and stops immediately when you toggle it off or disconnect.

Network (Internet, Wi-Fi state, network state, Wi-Fi multicast) — used to discover and connect to the desktop companion on your local Wi-Fi via mDNS (Bonjour) and to maintain the TCP/UDP connection that carries trackpad, keyboard, media, and audio data. Internet access is also used by Google Play Billing (see §3), to send crash reports if they are on (see §3), to ask our website once per launch whether a newer version of the app exists (this sends nothing about you — it fetches a small file listing the latest version, and can be turned off in Settings → Privacy → Check for updates) and, only if you ask the app to email you the desktop download link, by the email delivery service.

Motion sensors — the optional Air Mouse mode reads the gyroscope on-device to move the pointer. Sensor data is not stored and is only sent as pointer movement to your own computer.

3. Third-party services

Google Play Billing (in-app purchases) — if you make an in-app purchase, the transaction is handled by Google Play (or the Apple App Store on iOS). We receive a confirmation to unlock the purchased feature, but we do not receive or store your payment-card details. Google's handling of payment data is governed by Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Google Play Services — the app uses Google Play Services and Google ML Kit (for QR decoding). These are governed by Google's privacy policy.

Google Firebase Crashlytics (crash reports) — when the app crashes or hits a serious error, it sends a report to Firebase Crashlytics, a Google service we use only to find and fix bugs. A report contains the technical details of the error (stack trace), your device model, operating system version, app version, free memory and storage, the time of the error, and a random installation identifier created by Crashlytics that is not linked to your name, account, or phone number. It never contains your trackpad input, keystrokes, audio, or anything else you do in the app. Reports are kept for 90 days. They are on by default; turn them off at any time in Settings → Privacy → Send crash reports. Firebase's handling of this data: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

Google Sign-In and Firebase (optional account) — signing in is optional and every feature works without it. If you sign in with Google, Google shares your name, email address and profile photo with us. We store them, with when you created your account and last signed in, in Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore) to provide your account, and for nothing else: they are not used for advertising, not shared, not sold, and not linked to your crash reports or to anything you do with your computer. Only you can read your profile. Delete your account at any time in Settings → Delete account, or see https://touchify-mouse.web.app/delete-account; your account and profile are deleted right away.

4. Data we do NOT collect

Unless you choose to sign in with Google (§3), we do not collect your name, email address or photo. We never collect: your phone number or postal address; passwords; photos, videos, contacts, calendar, files, or SMS; precise GPS location; health, biometric, or financial information; analytics or usage tracking. Crash reports (see §3) are the only diagnostics we receive.

5. Data on your device

The app stores small, non-sensitive preferences locally on your device (e.g. selected theme, pointer sensitivity, audio options, last connected computer, whether Pro was purchased). This data never leaves your phone and is removed when you uninstall the app.

6. Children's privacy

The app is not directed to children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will take steps to address it.

7. Security

Connections to your desktop companion happen over your own local Wi-Fi network. We recommend you use a trusted, password-protected Wi-Fi network. We do not use TLS for the local connection because the data never leaves the LAN; please ensure your home/office Wi-Fi is properly secured.

8. Your rights

If you signed in, you can see your name, email and photo in Settings, and delete your account and profile there at any time (Settings → Delete account). Without the app, request deletion at https://touchify-mouse.web.app/delete-account or by email, and we will delete it within 7 days. Crash reports are not linked to your identity; you can stop sending them in Settings → Privacy, and ask us to delete reports from your device by contacting us. To control Google Play data, use your device settings and your Google account controls (https://myaccount.google.com). To delete all app data on your device, go to Settings → Apps → TouchifyMouse → Storage → Clear Data, or simply uninstall the app.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be indicated by a revised "Last updated" date at the top. Continued use of the app after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

10. Contact

For questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy or how the app handles data: aitools.deventiatech@gmail.com
